How it works
🔮 Predict a rivalChoose one opponent and guess what they will play. Correct predictions earn +2.
✊ Make your moveLock in rock, paper, or scissors. Moves reveal together.
🏆 Majority + counter bonusWith 3+ players, each player whose move counters another move gets +1. Add those bonuses to each move total. A two-move tie goes to the move with the counter bonus; if all three moves tie after bonuses, the round is a draw.
🎲 Chaos roundsRound 2 doubles base points, round 4 reverses RPS, and round 6 rewards ties.

You need at least 2 players. Game state stays in this browser for now; the room layer can be connected to an online backend later.
