How it works
🔮 Predict a rivalChoose one opponent and guess what they will play. Correct predictions earn +2.
✊ Make your moveLock in rock, paper, or scissors. Moves reveal together.
🏆 Majority + minority counter bonusWith 3+ players, move totals count. You only get the +1 counter bonus when your move is in the minority AND it beats another move chosen that round. Example: 1 Rock vs 2 Scissors → the Rock player gets the minority counter bonus.
🎲 Chaos roundsRound 2 doubles base points, round 4 reverses RPS, and round 6 rewards ties.
⚡ Team Powers — one use per game 🪨 Rock Solid: Blocks negative prediction points and protects your positive points from Cut in Half that round. 📄 Copycat: Copies the highest positive points another player earns that round. ✂️ Cut in Half: Choose another player and cut their positive points earned that round in half, rounded down. 🎲 Team Unpredictable — Jack of All Trades: Randomly gets Rock Solid, Copycat, or Cut in Half for that game. Powers cannot be used in Round 4. If Rock Solid blocks Cut in Half, the Scissors player keeps the power for later.
