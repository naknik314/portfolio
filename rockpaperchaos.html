Quick guide

How Rock Paper Chaos works

New here? Here’s the whole game in one quick page.
🔮 Predict a rivalChoose an opponent and guess what they will play. Predictions can gain or lose points: a correct prediction earns +2, while a wrong prediction can lose 0–3 points. Prediction rules depend on the game mode your host chooses.
🪨 Make your moveChoose Rock, Paper, or Scissors. Everyone’s moves stay hidden until the reveal.
🏆 Majority pointsWith 3+ players, the single most-played move earns +1 for each player who chose it. If the majority is tied, there are no majority points.
⚡ Minority counter bonusA minority move earns +1 when it beats the single majority move. Example: 1 Rock vs 2 Scissors gives Rock the counter bonus.
🎲 Chaos roundsSpecial rounds can double points, turn predictions off, reverse the matchups, and finish with Winner Takes the Round.
💠 Chaos PointsEarn Chaos Points by completing games. Save them up and spend them on optional extras in the Game Lobby before a match begins.
💡 Already know how to play? Hide this guide when creating future games. You can still reopen it anytime from the Game Lobby.
⚡ Team PowersYour assigned team is based on your previous winning-move point totals. Whichever move has earned you the most winning-move points determines your team; if you do not have a clear leader yet, you are Team Unpredictable. Your team gives you one power to use once per game.
  • 🪨 Team Rock — Barricade: protects you from negative points and point-loss effects that round.
  • 📄 Team Paper — Reprint: copies the highest positive points another player earns that round.
  • ✂️ Team Scissors — Cut in Half: cuts another player’s positive round points in half.
  • 🎲 Team Unpredictable: gets one random Rock, Paper, or Scissors team power after the game starts.
🎯 Solo Mode: Solo Mode has its own Solo-specific team powers.

🎯 Solo Mode
Learn about Solo Mode Solo Mode is available only when you are the only player in the Game Lobby. It has 7 minigame rounds featuring Quick Draw, Match the Cards, Cup Shuffle, and a final Chaos Spinner. How you win depends on your current team. 🪨 Team Rock: land on Rock at least 4 out of 7 times. 📄 Team Paper: land on Paper at least 4 out of 7 times. ✂️ Team Scissors: land on Scissors at least 4 out of 7 times. 🎲 Team Unpredictable: finish without any move reaching a clear majority. A clear majority is 4 out of 7 times. Complete your team challenge to earn +10 Chaos Points.
🎲 Roll the Dice Mode
Learn about Roll the Dice Mode Let the dice choose your move for the entire 7-round game. Each round, roll a six-sided die: 1–2 = 🪨 Rock, 3–4 = 📄 Paper, and 5–6 = ✂️ Scissors. Predictions and Team Powers still work normally.
🤖 Chaos Bot Mode
Learn about the challenge Chaos Bot Mode only works when at least one computer rival is in the game. Chaos bots predict more often, time powers more strategically, choose stronger targets, and each bot can receive a swapped power. In Chaos Mode, a computer rival using Cut in Half can halve a target's TOTAL game score. Bots never see hidden moves or receive free points.
🕵️ Double Agent Mode
Learn about Double Agent Mode Choose a different team's regular Team Power for one game. Your actual team, stats, and community team do not change. If Power Swap is also active, your chosen Double Agent power becomes its swapped version.
⚡ Power Swap
Learn about swapped powers You can swap your Team Power for an alternate Team Power for 1 game. 🪨 Barricade → Stonewall — blocks everyone else's positive round points before they are awarded. Barricade cannot block Stonewall because this prevents a gain rather than causing a point loss. 📄 Reprint → Misprint — copies the strongest negative result another player earns that round to everyone except you. ✂️ Cut in Half → Cut Your Losses — cancels all negative points you earn that round. 🎲 Team Unpredictable gets the swapped version of its random power.
